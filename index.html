Day by day

1Osaka

Arrive in Osaka
  • Land at Kansai International Airport. Complete arrival formalities (the Visit Japan Web form is free).
  • Get a SIM or eSIM. Airport vending-machine SIMs are cheaper than eSIM.
  • Check in at Namba or Umeda. Evening at Dotonbori.

Airport to Osaka Station

  • Haruka Express, about 30 min, INR 1,000
  • Limited express or rapid train, about 45 min, INR 600
  • Limousine bus, INR 1,000
  • Taxi, INR 6,000 to 8,000
2Osaka

Osaka sightseeing
  • Osaka Castle and Park, then Abeno Harukas 300 (9 AM to 10 PM).
  • Shinsekai district and Tsutenkaku Tower.
  • Tennoji Park if time allows.
Tip: Get the Osaka Amazing Pass to explore the whole city — that's what we did. It covers public transport for the day plus free entry to most major attractions, so it pays for itself fast on a day like this.
3Osaka

Universal Studios or Hiroshima
  • Pick one of the two day trips below.

Choose one

  • Universal Studios Japan: Studio Pass INR 5,000
  • Hiroshima and Miyajima: bus tour from INR 10,500, or INR 25,000 with Shinkansen from Osaka
4Osaka → Kyoto

Travel to Kyoto
  • Leave around 12:30 PM to check in at about 3 PM.

Ways to Kyoto

  • Shinkansen from Shin-Osaka, about 15 min
  • Thunderbird Limited Express, about 40 min, covered by JR Pass
  • Hankyu Limited Express from Osaka Umeda, about 1 hour, about INR 300, not covered by JR Pass
5Kyoto

Historic Kyoto
  • Sannenzaka and Kiyomizu-dera early in the morning.
  • Ninenzaka, the Ghibli store, and Gion.
  • Kodai-ji Temple.

Optional

  • Kimono rental and tea ceremony
6Kyoto

Nara
  • Rokumei Coffee, Nakatanidou, Kofukuji, Nara Deer Park, Todaiji.
  • Lunch, Cafe Nagomi, Yoshikien Garden. Return to Kyoto.
7Kyoto

Arashiyama
  • Bamboo Forest.
  • Otagi and Adashino Nenbutsu-ji.
  • Village walk and Coffee Java.
8Kyoto

Fushimi Inari and Kamogawa
  • Fushimi Inari Taisha.
  • Vermillion Cafe and Wife and Husband Cafe.
  • Sunset at Kamogawa Park.
9Kyoto → Tokyo

Kyoto to Tokyo
  • Check out and take a Shinkansen (about 2 hours).
  • Check in around 3 to 4 PM.

Ways to Tokyo

  • Nozomi, Hikari or Kodama. Nozomi is about INR 8,800.
  • With a JR Pass, use Hikari or Kodama.
10Tokyo

Tokyo highlights
  • teamLab Planets, Imperial Palace gardens, Meiji Jingu.
  • Takeshita Street, Hachiko, Shibuya Sky, Scramble Crossing.

Optional

  • Shibuya go-karting, INR 3,650 (International Driving Permit needed)
11Tokyo

Mount Fuji (weather dependent)
  • Go only on a clear day. Check the live camera first.
  • Stops: Kawaguchiko, Oishi Park or Oshino Hakkai, Hikawa Clock Shop, Chureito Pagoda.

Ways to go

  • Group tour, INR 4,600 to 51,000. Usually 8 AM to about 6 PM.
  • Self-planned: highway bus from Shinjuku, INR 1,275, or Fuji Excursion, INR 2,044
12Tokyo

Asakusa, Skytree and Akihabara
  • Senso-ji and Tokyo Skytree.
  • Akihabara and Yodobashi Akiba.
  • Optional: Shinjuku and Tokyo Tower.
13Tokyo

Theme park
  • Pick one of the two below.

Choose one

  • Tokyo Disney Resort (Disneyland or DisneySea), INR 5,000
  • Warner Bros. Studio Tour Tokyo, INR 4,052
14Tokyo

Ginza shopping
  • Onitsuka Tiger, Uniqlo, GU, MUJI, Loft and Don Quijote.
  • Cafes and Indian food in Ginza.
15Departure

Fly home
  • Check out and head to Haneda Airport.

To Haneda

  • Keikyu Line from Shinagawa, INR 295
  • Tokyo Monorail from Hamamatsucho

Nationwide JR Pass

Cards and where they work

  • Suica: Tokyo region. Use it if you start in Tokyo.
  • ICOCA: Osaka and Kansai. Use it if you start in Osaka.
  • PASMO: Tokyo region, an alternative to Suica.
  • IC cards work on trains, buses, konbini and vending machines. They do not replace Shinkansen reservations.
  • Suica and ICOCA: pay 1,000 yen, get 500 yen to use, minimum balance 300 yen.
  • Taxis cost about INR 250 per km. Use GO or Uber.

Travel tips

Good to know

Etiquette

  • Learn: Konnichiwa, Arigatou gozaimasu, Sumimasen.
  • Queue neatly and quietly. Use the marked lines on platforms.
  • Bow instead of shaking hands. Use both hands for money, cards and gifts.
  • Remove shoes at ryokan, temples and some restaurants.
  • Keep your voice down on trains and phones on silent. Avoid calls on trains.
  • Do not tip. Do not eat while walking. Do not point fingers.
  • Do not stab chopsticks upright in rice. Do not jaywalk.
  • Avoid unsolicited photos of locals, especially children and inside temples.

Getting around and money

  • Carry cash. Many small shops and rural places take only yen. Cash is preferred for IC top-ups.
  • Use Google Maps for trains and buses. Pick the right station exit to avoid extra walking.
  • Book reserved Shinkansen seats early. Some have space for large luggage.
  • On buses, Kyoto is back-in and front-out; Tokyo is the reverse.
  • Each station has an information centre if you are lost.
  • Keep a power bank and a Type A/B plug adapter.

Food and shopping

  • Vegan and vegetarian food is widely available. Konbini are cheap and good.
  • Slurping noodles is fine. Use oshibori for your hands only.
  • Carry a small bag for rubbish. Bins are rare.
  • Carry your passport when shopping for tax-free purchases on the spot.
  • Jain travellers: carry packaged food from India.

Planning

  • Avoid July and August because of the weather.
  • Mid-March to mid-May is the most expensive period (cherry blossom).
  • Book popular activities and peak-season hotels early.
  • Choose a hotel close to a station, and check room size in sq. metres.
  • Read reviews before booking. Not every hyped restaurant is worth the queue.
  • Do not skip Nara.

  • Download the Tokyo Disney Resort app and select your park.
  • Add an international credit card once inside the park, with international transactions enabled.
